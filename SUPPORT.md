# AuroraRGB - Support

AuroraRGB controls the RGB lighting and fans on your PC, with no ads, accounts, subscriptions or telemetry.

## Getting help

Email **nzbennycohen@gmail.com**. Please include:

- your motherboard, and the lighting devices you have
- what you were doing when the problem happened
- what you expected to happen instead

## Common questions

**Which hardware does it support?**
Gigabyte motherboards with the ITE 5702 RGB controller, the Logitech PRO X TKL Rapid, the MSI MPG 271QRX and Corsair Vengeance RGB DDR5 (AMD systems). Anything else is left alone.

**Why don't I see my motherboard fans or CPU temperature?**
They need admin rights and the free PawnIO driver from pawnio.eu. Install PawnIO, then click "Restart as admin" on the Fans tab.

**My lights flicker or fight with another app.**
Other lighting apps (for example a keyboard or motherboard vendor's app, or Windows Dynamic Lighting) can control the same lights. Close them or turn their lighting off.

**Does it need the internet?**
No. AuroraRGB makes no network connections.
