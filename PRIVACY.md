# AuroraRGB privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

AuroraRGB is made by Benny Cohen.

## What AuroraRGB collects
Nothing. AuroraRGB has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry. It makes no network connections.

## What stays on your PC
Your settings, profiles, imported effects and a short device log are saved on your own PC, in the app's data folder. They are never sent anywhere.

## Hardware access
AuroraRGB talks to supported lighting devices over USB, and, if you choose "Restart as admin", reads fan speeds and temperatures and sets fan speeds through the PawnIO driver. This happens on your PC only.

## Children
AuroraRGB collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
